🚒 WNY 3RD ALARM | FIRE BUFFS

Western New York Fire Photography & Incident Awareness

Welcome to the official GitHub repository for WNY 3RD ALARM, a fire buffing and emergency photography group based in Western New York.

Our mission is to document emergency apparatus, share photography, promote public safety awareness, and support our local first responders.
