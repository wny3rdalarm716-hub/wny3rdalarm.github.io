About WNY 3rd Alarm

WNY 3rd Alarm Fire Buffs is a Western New York-based group brought together by an interest in fire apparatus, emergency-service photography, and the traditions of the fire service. We aim to share respectful, responsible coverage and celebrate the work of local emergency services.

Our standards

Safety comes first

We are observers and photographers—not emergency responders. We do not self-dispatch into incidents or enter restricted areas.

We photograph only from lawful public locations or where we have explicit permission. We follow instructions from emergency personnel, never obstruct operations, never trespass, and never drive distracted. We do not publish private, identifying, or unverified incident information.
